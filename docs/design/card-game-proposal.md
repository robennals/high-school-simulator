# Proposal: everything is a card game

**Status: superseded by [the card rules spec](card-rules-spec.md).** Rob asked for "no mysterious multipliers…
all social interactions happen by people playing cards… everything else you
choose has effect in terms of cards". This is a design for that, for review.

**Summary.** Everyone — you and all 200 students — plays the same card game
with the same printed rules. Every effect on a relationship is a small whole
number printed on a card or on a rule card. Everything you choose outside a
conversation (personality, clothes, activity, evenings) changes your cards:
which cards you hold, what they cost, or a **modifier card** you have in play.
Nothing happens through a number you can't see the rule for. What stays hidden
is the *other players' hands and choices* (like any card game) and some dice.

## 1. What you can see about each person

Three small numbers, toward you, shown as pips:

| | Range | What it means | Built mainly by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ♥ Like | −5 … +5 | how much they like you | warm cards, shared opinions |
| ★ Respect | −5 … +5 | how much they look up to you | showing skill, staying calm |
| 🤝 Trust | 0 … 5 | how much they'd confide in / stand by you | sharing, helping, standing up for them, time outside school |

- **Friend** = ♥ 3+. **Close friend** = ♥ 3+ and 🤝 3+. These are printed
  thresholds, not hidden formulas.
- **Standing** (popularity) is public: ⭐ 1–5, worked out each Sunday from how
  much ★ the school gives you (with a printed rule, e.g. "your Standing is
  your rank by total ★, in fifths").
- **Still hidden until revealed:** you see "?" for a person's ♥★🤝 until
  they've reacted or spoken to you (as now). Other players' hands are hidden.

## 2. A conversation

- Everyone at the table takes turns playing one card (as now), you included.
  Other students play from their own decks by simple, stated habits
  (e.g. "warm people mostly play warm cards").
- **Energy ⚡** (as now): cards cost 0–2; you have up to 10 per day (smaller
  numbers read better; same balance as 20 halved).
- **Every card prints its exact effect.** Examples:

  | Card | ⚡ | Effect |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Ask about them | 1 | Target ♥ +1. About something they love (their shirt, a thing they told you): ♥ +2. |
  | Really listen *(response: after they tell you something)* | 0 | Target ♥ +1. After they shared something personal: also 🤝 +1. |
  | Compliment | 1 | Target ♥ +1. If their ⭐ is 2+ above yours: everyone watching ★ −1 (sucking up). |
  | Share something small | 1 | If target ♥ ≥ 1: 🤝 +1. Otherwise ♥ −1 (too much, too soon). |
  | Open up | 2 | If target ♥ ≥ 3: 🤝 +2, ♥ +1. Otherwise ♥ −1. |
  | Love/Diss: a topic | 1 | Each listener who agrees ♥ +1; who disagrees ♥ −1. |
  | Talk (a hobby) | 1 | Fans of it: ★ +1. Everyone else: no effect. |
  | Crack a joke | 2 | Roll: lands on 1–N in 6 (N = your Humor). Lands: everyone ♥ +1. Flops: everyone ★ −1. |
  | Tease | 2 | If target ♥ ≥ 2: ♥ +1, 🤝 +1. Otherwise target ♥ −2 and onlookers who like them ♥ −1. |
  | Brag | 2 | Listeners who value that skill ★ +1; everyone else ♥ −1. |
  | Offer help | 1 | Target 🤝 +1, ★ +1, and they owe you a **Favor card**. |
  | Gossip | 1 | Everyone ♥ +1 now; you get **Gossip −1**: your stories are believed less. |
  | Put someone down | 2 | See §5. |
  | Invite to hang out | 2 | They say yes if their ♥ ≥ 3 (♥ ≥ 2 with an easy out). Yes: after school together. No in public: you ★ −1. |

- **Dice, not formulas.** Where luck matters (a joke landing, a tryout,
  getting caught behind the gym) the card shows the odds as "N in 6".

## 3. Your choices become cards

| You choose | It becomes |
| --- | --- |
| **Abilities** (point buy) | Cards in your deck and your odds: Humor 4 → two joke cards, jokes land 4 in 6. Best skill → Brag and Help cards. |
| **Personality** (five sliders → pick a side, or stay in the middle) | A **trait card** always in play, e.g. *Outgoing*: cards said to everyone cost 1 less ⚡. *Reserved*: Really listen gives an extra ♥ +1. *Warm*: your Compliments never read as sucking up. *Edgy*: Tease and Push back cost 1 less ⚡. *Sensitive*: you see everyone's ♥ immediately, but put-downs cost you 2 ⚡. *Thick-skinned*: put-downs cost you nothing. *Organized*: Homework gives an extra grade step. *Curious*: one extra Ask. |
| **Look** | A modifier card: *Dressing like a crowd* — the first time you meet someone with your look, their ♥ +1. |
| **Shirt** | A modifier card for the day, printed with who it brings and who it invites to tease: *Funny tee* — people who value funny may open with Ask about your shirt (♥ +2); edgy people may Tease it. *Fan tee* — fans of X may open with you; people who hate X may mock it. *Hoodie* — no one opens with you; Teases at you cost 1 ⚡ more. *Plain tee* — nothing either way. |
| **Activity** | Where you play every afternoon (same people → more chances), and +1 copy of that skill's cards after every 2 weeks. |
| **Evenings** | **Day cards** with printed effects: Homework (grades +1 step), Practice (+1 skill card, or hobby card), Friends (a short private conversation with close friends), Rest (+3 ⚡ tomorrow), Phone (draw a Gossip card now, −2 ⚡ tomorrow). Repeating a day card two nights in a row halves its effect (printed). |

## 4. Things that happen between conversations — also printed rules

- **Gossip spreads as cards.** A story is a card other students hold and play;
  when someone hears it, the story card's printed effect applies to how they
  see the people in it.
- **Opinions drift.** Rule card: "Each Sunday, each person's opinion moves one
  step toward what most of their close friends think."
- **Friendships fade.** Rule card: "Each Sunday, ♥ drops by 1 toward 0 for
  anyone you haven't talked to that week."
- **Standing** is recomputed each Sunday by its printed rule (§1).

## 5. Put-downs and standing up, as cards

A put-down is a card played at you; your reply is a response card:
Don't take the bait / Turn it into a joke / Walk away / Explain yourself /
Snap back, each with its printed effect on ★ for you and the bully (e.g.
*Don't take the bait*: bully ★ −1 from everyone watching; you ★ +1). Being
stressed removes the good replies from your hand (printed: "Stressed: discard
your calm responses").

## 6. What this changes, honestly

- **The simulation underneath gets simpler.** Today relationships are
  decimals with research-based effects (first impressions count more,
  closeness grows slowly, conformity cascades, PageRank-style status). Those
  become printed rules with small numbers. Some subtlety is lost; in return
  everything is learnable. Each research effect that matters gets its own
  printed rule (e.g. "first impressions count double").
- **Realism has to be re-tuned.** The realism check (close friends per
  student, mutual friendships, bullying not paying off, mixes beating single
  habits) will be re-run, and the printed numbers adjusted until it holds.
- **Size:** this rewrites how the simulation applies effects (most of
  `perceive.ts`), the card definitions, and the rulebook. Roughly a few
  sessions of work, done in stages so the game stays playable.

## Open decisions

1. Integers for ♥ ★ 🤝 (−5…+5) instead of today's −100…100.
2. Other students play the very same cards by stated habits (symmetric game).
3. Personality as trait cards: five sliders become five two-sided choices.
4. Abilities as copies of cards plus printed dice odds.
5. Evenings and after school as day cards with printed effects.
6. Background processes (gossip, opinion drift, fading, standing) as printed
   rule cards.
7. Energy 10 per day instead of 20.
